# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who
takes part in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere else you represent the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, identity or
  level of experience.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback
  gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language
  or imagery, or publishing other people's private information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and focus on what is best for the
  community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the
maintainer, Tanvir Ahamed ([@nettanvirdev](https://github.com/nettanvirdev)):

- For problems you are comfortable raising in public, open an issue.
- For anything sensitive, use GitHub's
  [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
  feature on the comment, issue or pull request concerned, or open a minimal
  issue asking the maintainer to contact you privately, without details.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. The maintainer
may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants, as described in the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.
